Aha!

Posted on July 25, 2003 by Geoff Honnor
Posted in Uncategorized

Is plucky, Baghdad blogging, man of mystery, Salam Pax really Robert Fisk!?

Scroll down to read this observation:

"At that press conference there was a gentleman who asked an extremely important question which was answered by Sanchez with "that is speculation. Next question." I later found out that the man in front of me was Fisk and the question he asked which we all want to be answered was: why was the decision made to attack [Qusay and Uday] with a force that would have been capable of annihilating a city block? Why did they opt for killing them when they knew their importance as sources of information on all sorts of things and the wish all Iraqis have that they be put thru trial?

Fisk started the ball rolling, sanchez was asked the same question at least 5 times in different ways and with it the question of how to prove this to the Iraqi people. And what do we get? Meaningless militareses. Beyond disappointing."

Hmmmm......extremely important question eh?
