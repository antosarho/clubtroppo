I Get Too Hungry For Dinner At 8..

Posted on November 2, 2003 by Geoff Honnor
Posted in Uncategorized

So.....it's Una's Restaurant, 340 Victoria St, Darlinghurst on Tuesday 4 November at 6.30pm. Troppo bloggers past and present are invited to join Great Armadillo Ken Parish, and I, for dinner. You don't have to be a Troppo blogger to join us - after all, most people who'll be present at Una's on Tuesday night won't know a 'Troppo Armadillo' from that useless nag they drew earlier in the office sweepstake.

Chris, Roop, Stephen - would be good to get a nay or yay in advance so that we don't have ugly scenes with overwrought people in large hats trying to steal the seats we'll be hoarding for you at the table. Wendy and Wayne - we'll think fondly of you.

PS: This is not to be confused with the already notorious Sydney Bloggers Bash scheduled for Saturday 8 November. The ever-changing details of that bacchanal are available under "Going Down South" elsewhere on Troppo.
