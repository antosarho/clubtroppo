Welcome to Don Arthur

Posted on October 7, 2004 by Ken Parish
Posted in Uncategorised

As you can see from the post immediately below, Don Arthur has joined the Troppo blogging mob. Don is a longstanding stop-and-start blogger, due to the demands of employment and postgraduate study and research. His most recent blog is here, but Don found he was unable to update it frequently enough for viability, and has decided joining a group blog like Troppo would be a better bet.

Don has always been one of my favourite bloggers, and his patchy presence at Troppo is more than welcome. I love Don's whimsical, analytical style with a wry po-mo twist, and I hope you will too.

Incidentally, one of the main reasons I developed Troppo as a group blog in the first place was precisely to provide a blogging outlet for excellent writers who just didn't have the time or inclination to maintain their own solo blog. Don's move fits perfectly with that concept, as does the fact that Geoff Honnor and Wendy James are posting infrequently at the moment. With a bit of luck both may find a bit more time for blogging in the near future. And I wonder what's happened to Wayne Wood?
