Skateboarding rules!

Posted on November 18, 2004 by Sophie Masson
Posted in Sport-general

Well, the SMH is playing host again to a Masson family piece! This time, it's actually a Masson-Leach piece, our youngest son, 15 year old Bevis, writing an opinion piece about the joys of skateboarding, free of the controls of well-meaning programs like the government-funded Street Active program. You can find it on the Sydney Morning Herald's website too, in their Opinions section.. What needing to earn money can do for a child's initiative! He desperately wanted to go to a watersports summer camp in January but as we'd already paid for him to go to a skiing one in winter, we told him he had to earn his own money towards it. And this piece did it!
