Left Right Out

Posted on December 10, 2004 by Mark Bahnisch
Posted in Uncategorized, Humour

Internet quizzes are always fun. Except for those pesky iq tests... Via internet quizzes, I first found out that I was 47% slutty, and that there was an 80% probability that I was a woman.

Courtesy of Alex in a comment on a Catallaxy thread, here's a more serious internet quiz with what I think is a reasonable questionnaire (albeit a tad Americo-centric). Locate yourself on the Political Compass.

I'm a Left Libertarian and my scores were: Economic Left/Right: -8.12
Social Libertarian/Authoritarian: -7.74. That's about right, I think... (Only the Dalai Lama comes close to me among famous figures. I don't know whether he did the test, though...)

TROPPO CONTEST: The person who comes closest to Troppo faux centrism will win a mystery Troppo prize.
