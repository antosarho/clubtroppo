A Very Troppo Christmas

Posted on December 24, 2004 by Mark Bahnisch
Posted in Uncategorized, Uncategorised, Life

Revellers on a Hot Hill End Night

That's me in the middle. I don't normally wear Hawaiian shirts but it's a good troppo look.

Well, all the presents are bought and only the grog remains (though I made a start on the bubbles on my partner's back deck last night relaxing with a nice view of the city through the gum trees...). Looks like the Brisvegas weather is going to be troppo indeed!.

Anyway, I'd like to wish Troppo readers and my fellow writers a very happy Chrissie - and a holy one for those of you so inclined. Remember - play safe, kids & I'll see you when I surface!
