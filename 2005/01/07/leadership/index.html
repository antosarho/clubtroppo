    Steve E., "My instinct...would pose a major deterrence to people claiming land rights''

    The major deterrence is proof of un-interrupted traditional occupation of land since 1788, a difficult enough task in itself indeed. On the other hand, with no legislation, Aboriginal people could have squatted on any vulnerable assets of mining or leasehold pastoralist's land forcing the latter to take them to court with the outcomes not being determined for years or decades. Legislation was therefore clearly necessary and Keating avoided the chaos that otherwise would have arisen.

    You have also made no account of State legislation which still would have been made despite your posited ''do nothing'' federal response. The last thing the States wanted was their Supreme Courts clogged up with Mabo common law cases.

    ''Keating continued a disastrous policy with intermittent bi-partisan support''

    Saying that something is disastrous without argument certainly does not prove your point which is your opposition to Aboriginal people having land rights. What was so wrong with the Native Titles Act which aimed to 1) recognise and protect Native Title, 2) establish ways in which future acts affecting Native Title could proceed, and to set standards for them. 3) Establishing a mechanism for determining claims to Native Title and 4) To validate past acts that are invalid due to the existence to Native Title.

    What could be more reasonable than that in the light of Mabo and faced with chaos if nothing was done legislatively? Where are the large tracts of land taken over by native title that threaten tenure for miners and leaseholders?

    '' We should go back to first principles and start again''

    What first principles? Terra Nullius? What does it take to convince you that the overturning of Terra Nullius is set in stone, and Aboriginal land rights are here to stay?

    Are you not in grave danger of falling into c.s.'s trap of ''conspicuous indignation'' over something you or anybody else can never change back to the pre-1993 Mabo decision?
