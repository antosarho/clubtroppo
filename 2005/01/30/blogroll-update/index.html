Blogroll Update

Posted on January 30, 2005 by Ken Parish
Posted in Uncategorized, Uncategorised

...is now done. There are a lot of new blogs added to the leftish and centrist categories. 15 new leftish links and 4 centrist ones. It's interesting to note how many of the new blogs are written by women and also by people in their 20s. As opposed to us thirty something blokes, I guess. Another common factor in people's biographies is that there are quite a few lapsed Catholics, quite a few ex-student politicians and quite a few former party members.

Please feel free to report any broken links in comments.

ELSEWHERE: The debate about blogs, awards and politics continues at James Russell's place.

UPDATE: I've also added Stack to the 'specialist blogs' category. It's a book blog owned by Georg of Terminological Inexactitude fame. I'm inclined to agree with her tagline, "There's more to life than books you know, but not much more." Additionally, I've linked there to Amanda's music blog Flop Eared Mule - country music with a Sydney focus.
