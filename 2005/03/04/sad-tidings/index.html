Sad Tidings

Posted on March 4, 2005 by Mark Bahnisch
Posted in Uncategorized, Uncategorised, Life

Frequent Troppo commenter and proprietrix of her own blog, yellowvinyl, who's a friend of mine, rang me today to let me know that she has cancer. She asked me to pass on her apologies for being testy in comments threads, which I'm sure are wholly unnecessary in any case. She's just posted about this on her livejournal site. Since yellow is a regular at Troppo, I'm sure all readers will join me in wishing her a full recovery.

  11. yellowvinyl
    March 4, 2005

    Thx everyone for yr kind words :) and thoughts and energy and prayers :)

    Ken's right - again is worrying. I had osteocarcoma (a bone cancer) when I was 14 which led to the amputation of one leg. that's what it is this time too - in the other leg. fortunately the diagnosis has been relatively early - these cancers are fast spreading - and there are no secondary tumours. I'd been feeling listless and unwell for a while - but I put it down to overwork and too much partying... and my leg was very sore but that's not so unusual when you've only got one. so I'm glad having been through all this once before motivated me to get it checked out. Osteocarcoma doesn't generally respond well to chemo, and I'm still getting advice on treatment options (the treatment when I was a girl was basically amputation to get all the cells that might be problematic) and it sounds as if they might be able to excise the affected part of the bone, but there's still a possibility that I'll end up legless in the proper sense not in the sense that I regularly am on a Friday night in a club :( but I'm holding up reasonably well so far, though obviously feeling very unwell.

    I'm also grateful that I have income insurance. when you work for yourself as I do there's no sick leave...

    James, I missed the OC this week - please distract me by filling me in on all the juicy goss!

    and thanks, Nabs :)

    I shouldn't have stayed up last night - am paying the price today!

    I went to see the specialist yesterday to get the lowdown on what's happening. the good news is that the range of treatments, survival rates, and strategies for avoiding amputation is much bigger than when I was 14 and went through this the last time round. the bad news (personally in terms of how I'll feel) is that chemo is now thought to be effective - am looking at six to nine months of same. I might do a pre-emptive strike and go for the bald look now! half way through the chemo they operate. it's at that point, depending on whether the tumour's shrunk that they decide whether to excise part of the bone and replace it either with a bone graft or a prosthetic bone replacement or to amputate. if the latter is necessary, the location of the tumour means I'd likely get to keep my knee - which would be a big advantage in mobility options - not easy for a double above-knee amputee to walk on prosthetics. but hopefully it won't come to that. at the moment, I'm in a wheelchair which I am not enjoying.
