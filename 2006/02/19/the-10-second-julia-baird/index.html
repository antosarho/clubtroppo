The 10 second Julia Baird

Posted on February 19, 2006 by Don Arthur
Posted in IT and Internet

Writers should keep it short and get to the point, says Julia Baird. Text messaging shows that the Sesame Street generation and generation Y get this.

That's the gist of Julia Baird's latestGood Weekend column -- 'Brief Encounters'. What a pity there was so much space on the page left to fill. Julia was forced to tell a 4 paragraph story about a friend who painted his dog's toenails before getting the vet to put it down, quote a Judith Wright poem, drop a reference to Cyrano de Bergerac and refer to a Washington Post story which referenced a study by Tegic Communications.
