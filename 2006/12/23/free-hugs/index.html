Free hugs

Posted on December 23, 2006 by Nicholas Gruen
Posted in Uncategorised, Life

I was watching Rage while polishing off a bedtime snack and saw a video that I though was going to annoy me - but turned out to amuse me - and indeed to make me smile and feel good. And of course it's on You Tube. The city it's filmed in turns out to be Sydney as I realised when I saw Darling Harbour in the background. Have a watch.
