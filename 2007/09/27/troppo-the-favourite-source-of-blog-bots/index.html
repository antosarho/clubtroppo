Posted in Uncategorised, Site News

I've just become aware of bot blogging curtesy of thesethreelinks to posts I've just put up. I guess they're part of the escalating SEO war. Anyway, if you look below the fold, my hope is that some geek will have explained what it's all about, whether and the extent to which we should be alarmed, shocked or outraged and who we should be writing to to have it all fixed - if indeed there is something wrong.

    Geekfree moment. We have noticed similar automated aggregating pings/trackbacks, but not from these three (which look as if they come from the same person).
