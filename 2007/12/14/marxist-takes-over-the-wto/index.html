Marxist takes over the WTO

Posted on December 14, 2007 by Nicholas Gruen
Posted in Economics and public policy, Philosophy, Politics - international

Well blow me down this is true. From this website director general of the World Trade Organization, Pascal Lamy is happy to have himself described as a Marxist. Might not be the most low key way to get his message across. Anyway, if he hadn't described himself as a Marxist, it would be hard to tell him from a Social Democrat (which he also claims to be). He likes Rawls and doesn't like the French left calling Rawls a 'right wing' philosopher.
