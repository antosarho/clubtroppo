Youth (Opening Night)


Two old friends vacation at a prestigious hotel in the Swiss Alps. Fred is a suave socialite and retired composer who the British royal family is pestering to play again. Mick is a film director rushing to complete a screenplay. Together they muse sympathetically and with curiosity at those that surround them; including their children’s confused love lives, Mick’s enthusiastic young writers, and the hotel guests.

Ryan's Daughter


Loosely based on Madame Bovary, this overlooked masterpiece follows Rosy, resident of a small Irish village and daughter of local innkeeper Tom Ryan. Rosy is unhappily married to a drab schoolmaster Charles Shaughnessy, who is completely against type. Edgy and unsatisfied, she begins a dangerous affair with British officer Randolph Doryan, despite the tension of Wartime and the threat of her nationalist neighbours finding out. Her indiscretion has far reaching consequences however, leading to tragedy and ultimately disgrace.

Dark Horse: The Incredible True Story of Dream Alliance


Dark Horse: The Incredible True Story of Dream Alliance is an inspirational true story of Welsh racing glory branching from the village of Cefn Fforest, one of the poorest mining valleys in Wales. When Jan Vokes, a barmaid at a local working men’s club, overhears a regular discuss his part ownership of a racehorse, she decides to breed one of her own. Going into business with the man and her husband Brian she purchases a thoroughbred mare and pairs her with an aging stallion. To raise the foal, they unite with 23 friends in the village who form a syndicate paying just £10 a week. To the astonishment of the racing elite, the horse, aptly named Dream Alliance grows up to be an unlikely champion. The victorious times do not last however, and the glory takes a disastrous turn.

Women in Love


Close friends Rupert and Gerald begin a relationship with two sisters; the schoolteacher Ursula and the free-spirited artist Gudrun with whom they share a morbidly surreal affection. After a shockingly bizarre incident, they eventually wed, taking a joint honeymoon to Switzerland. Infidelity and death mars their joyous holiday however, and in the midst of this madness the aloof Gerald and the eccentric Gudrun embark on sexual exploration as both try to comprehend the notion of a perfect relationship.

The Prime of Miss Jean Brodie


Based on a novel, then turned into a Tony award-winning play, The Prime of Miss Jean Brodie is the story of a teacher who encourages romantic and political idealism in her precocious students, educating them in matters of love and art over cold hard facts. Her controversial teaching style captures the attention and indignation of the school’s headmistress, Miss Mackey. Unfazed by this Miss Brodie falls into a complicated tryst with fellow teacher Mr. Lowther. When her politics indirectly causes a tragic event, Jean begins to unfold, questioning everything she believes in.

Filmed in Supermarionation


A fascinating look at Gerry and Sylvia Anderson, the pioneers behind the style of live-action puppetry made famous by British TV hit Thunderbirds. The original creators Lady Penelope and Parker themselves present a sentimental, honest and in-depth account of the original team utilising rare archived footage as well as brand new interviews from the surviving cast and crew and clips from the shows themselves. The film explores the creative process which the team remark on with hilariously dry wit. There is always a sense of fun throughout, with ingenious recreations of the techniques used, and awe-inspiring pyrotechnic wizardry.

A Matter of Life and Death


British Air Force pilot Peter Carter is returning to England after a dangerous bombing mission that has left his plane badly damaged. When he believes he will not make the trip back to land, he contemplates death. Before he makes this fateful decision he contacts June, a radio operator that he will spend his last moments conversing with. Amazingly, Peter miraculously survives, and with a new lease on life returns to June and they fall in love. Complications arise when a divine messenger from heaven visits Peter, telling him that his survival was not part of the plan and a case for his mortality begins with judge, jury and executioner in tow!

Elstree 1976


Star Wars is a phenomenon that reshaped cinema forever, but it is a lesser known fact that it owes its enduring legacy to its very first days in humble Elstree Northern London. It is 1976 and George Lucas starts filming the cinematic canon here, but this documentary is not a simple making-of, it is a fascinating account that personally delves into ten on-screen extras lives. These people, unaware how big Star Wars would become, appear briefly in a movie that defined a new era of the Hollywood Blockbuster. This documentary paints an intimate portrait of them and follows their altered lives after being involved with the second biggest franchise in film history, an enduring legacy they carry with them today.

The Man Who Knew Infinity (Closing Night)


Based on the stunning and inspirational biography of a genuine mathematical genius in the early twentieth century, this closing night gem is a treasured retelling of Srinivasa Ramanujan’s life. Through sweeping period detail and compelling theory the film reiterates how important Ramanujan’s work was; following his life from meek beginnings in Madras to great renown in Cambridge where his knowledge proves both a blessing and a curse.The movie depicts how the Cambridge University Professor G.H. Hardy who, while initially resistant, is eventually inspired and captivated by the Indian mathematician’s ground breaking theories. The complex evolution of their friendship is beautifully realised and as they transcend race and culture through mutual respect and understanding, Ramanujan’s trailblazing theories shine through the ignorance and prejudice around him.
